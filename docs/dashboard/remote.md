# Remote

The **Remote** tab, `/?tab=remote`, is the Control tab's remote on a single screen: it fits the height of a phone, so nothing has to be scrolled to reach a button while watching.

## The screen

* **Keys along the top:** Panel, Source, Apps, Sound, Picture and Open a web page. Each opens a sheet from the foot of the screen with that control in it, and closes again on the key, the cross, the background or Esc. Panel holds screen off, screen on, the screensaver, power and reboot.
* **Touchpad or D-pad:** one at a time, in the space between the keys, chosen by the pair of buttons above it. The choice is kept per browser.
* **Along the foot:** volume up, mute and volume down, then Back, Home and Settings, and the playback keys.

Volume and the D-pad's arrows repeat while held, as the remote's own keys do.

Each control here is the Control tab's own, moved to this tab while it is open, so there is one of each and both tabs always agree. The [Control](control.md) tab keeps everything, including the sleep timer, the screen notification, the energy saving steps and the OLED light.
